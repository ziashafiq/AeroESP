AeroESP Expert Review

Reviewing AI-generated questions on AeroESP.

Thank you for agreeing to help review AeroESP's AI-generated aerospace English questions. Here is exactly what to do.

1. Create an account as a Teacher

Go to the registration page and sign up. When asked for your role, choose Teacher. If you were given an invite code, enter it on this form.

2. Wait for approval

New teacher accounts are reviewed by an administrator before they become active - this usually takes a short time. You will see a "pending approval" message when you sign in until this happens; there is nothing further for you to do.

3. Sign in and open Expert Review

Once approved, sign in and look for Expert Review in the sidebar. This takes you to your review workspace, where AI-generated questions waiting for your feedback are listed.

4. What reviewing involves

For each assigned question, you will be asked to judge its technical accuracy (does the aerospace content hold up?), its English level and clarity, and whether the answer key is correct. Your judgment is what determines whether a question is trustworthy enough to use.

Confidentiality

The questions you review are unpublished and confidential. Please read the Terms of Use before you begin - it covers what is and is not permitted with this material.
